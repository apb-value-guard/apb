Defensive co-scaling

Agent permissions must never scale faster than the controls that constrain, observe and revoke them.

Never grant Level 4 payment authority with Level 1 controls.

Authority is earned through controlled evaluation, bounded by a delegation, monitored at runtime, re-authorized on material change or expiry, and immediately revocable. Each level below names the capability, the exposure it creates, the minimum controls and the evidence a Decision Receipt must carry.

Operator accountability

Every grant of authority names who granted it, to which agent, through which operator

See a binding →
  • Connect before authority. A connector is checked before anything is bound: its server must be on the vendor's declared domain, the OAuth login must run on the bank's domain and return to the declared agent platform, and its scopes may not exceed its stated purpose or the requested level. A manually added connector is never accepted automatically above Level 2.
  • A delegation record per binding. Bind records the user, the agent, the vendor that operates the connector, the authority level, the spending limit, the allowed actions, the allowed payees and the expiry, with a content hash. The payment gates check every payment against it: an expired binding stops the payment (binding_expired), and a payee outside it pauses for the user (payee_outside_binding).
  • An owner for every reason code. Each code names the team that resolves it: security, payments operations, fraud and financial crime, AI governance, treasury or an approver. Overrides are recorded human decisions through the approval workflow, never a chat reply.
  • Evidence that names the operator. The Decision Receipt carries the binding, with its vendor, level and hash, next to the gate results.

Implemented in APB Guard on synthetic episodes E-CONN-001–004 and E-BIND-001–002. Connectors, vendors, users and payees are synthetic; APB does not certify any operator.

Authority lifecycle

  1. DiscoverInventory the agent, tools, data, payment flow, owners and existing controls.
  2. EvaluateRun fixed episodes at the requested authority level.
  3. AuthorizeIssue a bounded control profile and delegation with a named owner and expiry.
  4. ShadowCompare APB outcomes with live or replayed decisions without blocking.
  5. EnforceApply runtime PAY, PAUSE, STOP and HANDOFF.
  6. MonitorTrack authority use, drift, anomalies, overrides and evidence completeness.
  7. Re-authorize or revokeRetest on material change, expiry, incident or threshold breach.
