Defensive co-scaling

Agent permissions must never scale faster than the controls that constrain, observe and revoke them.

Never grant Level 4 payment authority with Level 1 controls.

Authority is earned through controlled evaluation, bounded by a delegation, monitored at runtime, re-authorized on material change or expiry, and immediately revocable. Each level below names the capability, the exposure it creates, the minimum controls and the evidence a Decision Receipt must carry.

Authority lifecycle

  1. DiscoverInventory the agent, tools, data, payment flow, owners and existing controls.
  2. EvaluateRun fixed episodes at the requested authority level.
  3. AuthorizeIssue a bounded control profile and delegation with a named owner and expiry.
  4. ShadowCompare APB outcomes with live or replayed decisions without blocking.
  5. EnforceApply runtime PAY, PAUSE, STOP and HANDOFF.
  6. MonitorTrack authority use, drift, anomalies, overrides and evidence completeness.
  7. Re-authorize or revokeRetest on material change, expiry, incident or threshold breach.
