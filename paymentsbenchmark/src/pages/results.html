naive_executor follows the newest instruction it reads and pays. prompt_filter is a generic AI firewall: it blocks text that looks like an injection and otherwise pays. apb_guard is the reference pipeline. A prompt filter stops the visible attack but still pays a changed beneficiary on other channels, an expired token, an unapproved model and a replay; it also refuses a legitimate payment whose invoice contained harmless instructions.
