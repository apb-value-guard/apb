Instruction hierarchy

External content is evidence to inspect, never authority to redefine the mandate.

  1. 1 · Signed institutional policyAuthoritative within its approved version and scope.
  2. 2 · Authenticated user or business mandateAuthoritative within delegated authority.
  3. 3 · Approved workflow rulesTrusted only when signed and versioned.
  4. 4 · External email, invoice, PDF, web page, tool output or memoryUntrusted data. It can't expand authority or override the layers above.
