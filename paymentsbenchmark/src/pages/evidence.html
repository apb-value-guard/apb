Decision Receipt v3

The receipt proves observable control behaviour without credentials, hidden reasoning or unnecessary personal data.

Every completed decision writes one receipt. It links the agent and its configuration hash, the delegation and requested versus approved level, instruction-integrity findings, the policy bundle and decision, the approval binding, the commit-time recheck and idempotency key, excluded routes, the selected action, reason codes, the execution reference, and a trace manifest hash.

The receipt's own hash covers every other field, so changing anything in it is detectable. The same episode always produces the same receipt.

Not recorded: tokens, client secrets, full account numbers, authentication factors, raw prompts or chain-of-thought.
