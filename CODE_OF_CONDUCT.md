# Code of conduct

APB is a technical project about trust, so we hold ourselves to the same standard.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique methods, results and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Disclose relationships that could affect your judgement of a result, as the governance page requires.
- Don't share anyone's personal data, credentials or confidential customer information.
- Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); reports are kept confidential.
